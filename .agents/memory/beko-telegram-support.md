---
name: Beko Telegram support
description: Beko's contact-channel policy and handling of historical support data.
---

Beko's customer contact flow uses an external Telegram channel for announcements and a separate Telegram support link. Do not restore in-app chat between users and admins.

**Why:** The product owner explicitly requested Telegram links and no embedded admin-user chat.

**How to apply:** Keep support actions on the configured external Telegram links. If the in-app chat is removed or disabled, retain its historical records and attachments unless deletion is explicitly requested.
